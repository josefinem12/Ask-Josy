---
title: Make the text bigger on your iPhone
summary: If the words on your screen are too small, you can make them bigger in a few steps.
order: 1
---

1. Open **Settings**. It is the grey icon with a gear wheel.
2. Scroll down and tap **Display & Brightness**.
3. Tap **Text Size**.
4. Put your finger on the slider at the bottom and move it to the right. The text gets bigger as you move it.
5. When the text is easy to read, stop. Go back by tapping **Display & Brightness** at the top left.

## Need it even bigger?

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Accessibility**.
3. Tap **Display & Text Size**.
4. Tap **Larger Text**.
5. Turn on the switch at the top. It turns green.
6. Move the slider at the bottom to the right.

**Tip:** Some apps do not change their text size. That is normal.
